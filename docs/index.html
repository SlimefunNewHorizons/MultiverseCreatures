Wither Storm

It grows by eating the world

Nothing but block and item displays, laid out from the mod's own model code. Feed it and it evolves through five forms; each one is a different monster to fight.

See it in 3D

Orbit the selected form, play its roar and its head animations, and show its tractor beams and hitboxes.

About 1.5 MB, loaded only when you ask.

A tractor beam with a warningIt glows thin and harmless first, then pulls. Run sideways across the cone to break free.
Injure its headsProjectiles that reach a head injure it: the beam goes dark and it takes more damage. Injure all three and the storm is exposed.
It plays deadThe Destroyer and the Devourer fall with their jaws hanging, then rise with a roar and a blast. Only once.
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

The Arrow chooses

An Archer of the Arrow drops the Arrow; drink the Bearer's Elixir brewed from DIO's blood and it always awakens a Stand. Without it, the Arrow kills 70% of the time. Stands with a body are drawn with player heads: open them in the viewer.
