## ✨ What is MultiverseCreatures?

A Paper/Purpur/Spigot **1.21+** plugin that adds:

- 1 final boss with 5 phases and 55 attacks
- 2 minibosses (Mahoraga, Kinger)
- 14 themed creatures that replace natural spawns
- 1 rare Full-Moon military army trap
- 1 Wandering Trader replacement with custom trades
- 11 weapons, 3 armor pieces, 3 off-hand relics, 4 misc items, 2 food items
- 16 crafting components dropped from each mob
- Custom NBS music engine + ritual structures

Every vanilla mob spawn has a configurable chance to be replaced by an MSC counterpart; each counterpart has its own AI, drops specialized crafting ingredients, and grants access to themed legendary items.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. Install the JAR into `plugins/` (see [Installation](./Installation.md)).
2. Boot once to generate `config.yml`.
3. Tune spawn chances, cooldowns, boss stats as desired.
4. Use `/msc spawn <type>` to summon entities for testing, or just play — natural spawns will be replaced.
